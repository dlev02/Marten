# Bank provider options

Reviewed **2026-09-11** against current public documentation, rendered provider pages, and Marten's source. This is a research record, not confirmation of Drew's account entitlements. No provider account was created, agreement accepted, bank connected, or paid plan activated.

## Update: SimpleFIN Bridge (September 11, 2026)

[SimpleFIN Bridge](https://beta-bridge.simplefin.org/) is now Marten's second provider and the Sophtron pilot has been removed. The bridge is a consumer subscription (advertised at $1.50 + tax per month or $15 + tax per year, up to 25 institutions and 25 apps) that the user buys directly, links their banks in, and then authorizes apps with a one-time setup token. Each Marten user pastes their own token, so it needs no deployment secrets and works on a shared or self-hosted Marten. The [protocol](https://www.simplefin.org/protocol.html) defines balances and transactions. Marten also accepts bridge-supplied pending flags, merchant category codes and validated investment positions when present; those extensions are not guaranteed by the base protocol. Holdings supply quantities and values, with an implied unit price; ambiguous basis and quote timestamps remain unavailable. The bridge asks for at most about 24 requests a day and recommends 45-day windows. Implementation and limits: [simplefin.md](simplefin.md). SimpleFIN publishes no per-institution reliability data; validate a real subscription against the household's banks before relying on it.

Sophtron's research below is retained for context. Its personal-use license and key-sharing restrictions did not fit a shared deployment, and its API showed little recent activity, which is why the adapter was retired in favor of SimpleFIN.

## Recommendation

Keep Plaid for the operator's own household and offer SimpleFIN to everyone else. Drew and his dad can have separate Marten accounts while using the deployment's one Plaid configuration; their institution connections share its quota. For independently operated copies, each operator can configure their own provider account on their own backend, subject to that account's approval and terms.

Sophtron is a credible candidate for a **personal, separately configured second provider**: its current agreement explicitly offers free personal, noncommercial API use. Validate its data against the family's actual institutions before relying on it. Yodlee has the necessary aggregation capabilities, but its public material does not establish a current free live-data allowance or a price suitable for this small deployment.

Do not add a hosted “paste your free Plaid/Sophtron developer key” feature as a quota workaround. The public credential-sharing rules do not clearly authorize that arrangement. Provider-approved delegation or a commercial/partner arrangement would need to settle that first. This is an implementation recommendation from the documented restrictions, not a determination of what an unseen account agreement permits.

## How Plaid's limit works

An Item is one login at one institution and can contain several accounts. A checking account and three cards accessible under one Chase login can therefore be one Item. Drew's Chase login and his dad's separate Chase login are two Items. A Plaid client ID identifies the developer team, not an individual Marten user. [Plaid glossary](https://plaid.com/docs/quickstart/glossary/)

The current Trial applies to eligible US/Canada teams created on or after April 15, 2026. It allows **10 Production Items created in total**. Removing an Item does not recover a slot. Trial includes Transactions, Liabilities, and Investments. Pay-as-you-go has no minimum spend or commitment; exact prices require the account's pricing screen/agreement. Paid subscriptions attach to Items and products, and can continue without successful calls. Investments Holdings and Investments Transactions are separate subscriptions. Upgrading starts charges for initialized subscription products retained on the paid plan. [Plaid billing](https://plaid.com/docs/account/billing/)

Example: three institution logins for Drew and three for his dad consume six Trial Items, regardless of the number of accounts inside them. Previously created and removed Items still matter. Unlimited API calls against existing Trial Items do not increase the Item allowance or guarantee live data on every call. [Trial overview](https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/39994173227159-What-is-the-Plaid-Trial-plan)

Trial signup is available to US/Canada developers without an existing Production or Limited Production account. It does not require business registration, a security questionnaire, or a sales process. Identity verification, the standard MSA, and security attestations still apply; approval is not automatic for every applicant. Only US/Canadian institutions are available. [Trial eligibility and approval](https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/39994173227159-What-is-the-Plaid-Trial-plan)

The account's actual plan, creation date, consumed slots, product access, and production approval were not inspected in this review. Legacy Limited Production is a different offering; old advice about Development, 100 Items, or OAuth exclusions must not be applied to the new Trial. [Sandbox and live-data testing](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/)

## Hosted use, personal deployments, and credentials

| Arrangement                             | What it means for Marten                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One shared family deployment            | One server-side Plaid team configuration; separate users and Items. The operator's plan covers the combined usage. Bank consent remains individual.                                                                                                 |
| Independently operated personal copy    | Separate frontend, Convex project/deployment, auth setup, provider credentials, redirect registration, and webhook URL. Its operator controls its provider account and allowance. Hosting the frontend alone does not isolate provider credentials. |
| User-supplied keys in Drew's hosted app | Requires additional credential isolation, connection-to-credential binding, rotation, correct webhook routing, and permission from the provider for that model. Encryption alone does not establish contractual permission.                         |

Plaid's Developer Policy, effective April 19, 2026, prohibits sharing client credentials with third parties except Authorized Users with a legitimate business need. Authorized Users are employees, contractors, or agents acting for the account holder's benefit and approved use case. Being a Marten end user is not automatically that relationship. [Developer Policy](https://plaid.com/legal/#developer-policy)

Plaid does have an official reseller model with separate customer teams/keys, billing, and institution registration, but it requires a formal partnership. It is not a self-service free-key pooling mechanism. [Reseller documentation](https://plaid.com/docs/account/resellers/)

Sophtron's October 26, 2025 Developer Agreement licenses personal, noncommercial use; commercial use requires a separate license. It prohibits sharing the API key with third parties, supplies no support entitlement, and reserves revocation/update rights. Its end-user clauses do not unambiguously settle a shared family service or hosted BYOC. [Sophtron Developer Agreement](https://sophtron.com/developerAgreement)

Yodlee requires confidential client credentials on a secure server; its public docs do not supply a general hosted BYOC permission or personal-production entitlement. The applicable production agreement remains an account-specific question. [Yodlee environments and credential handling](https://developer.yodlee.com/resources/yodlee/json-web-token-authorization/docs/environments)

## Freshness and trust boundaries

Plaid transaction endpoints return its latest collected data, with collection frequency varying by institution; a Marten sync does not force the institution to refresh. Investments usually update at least daily on market days. [Transaction freshness](https://support.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/15373384920599-Do-the-transactions-get-and-transactions-sync-endpoints-provide-real-time-data), [Investments overview](https://plaid.com/docs/investments/)

Yodlee documents background refresh based on user activity, sometimes daily or every few days, plus data-update webhooks. Its performance guide specifically discourages continuous forced refreshes such as every four hours. [Refresh FAQ](https://developer.yodlee.com/resources/yodlee/faqs/docs/data_aggregation), [performance guidance](https://developer.yodlee.com/resources/yodlee/best-practices/docs/performance_improvements)

“Your bank credentials stay with your bank” is too broad. Plaid uses both OAuth/API connections and credential-based connections; it says bank credentials are never shared with the connected app. Use provider-specific, accurate copy at the connection step. [Plaid connection types](https://support-my.plaid.com/hc/en-us/articles/8117349507095-What-are-the-different-ways-third-party-apps-using-Plaid-can-connect-to-my-financial-accounts)

Sophtron's direct API can accept bank passwords and MFA answers. Its legacy OAuth sample uses a password grant with developer client credentials; that is not evidence of a modern end-user authorization-code/PKCE delegation flow suitable for hosted BYOC. Yodlee FastLink supports both provider redirects and credential-based sites. Prefer the provider-controlled connection interface after its exact credential path is verified. [Sophtron service client](https://github.com/sophtron/Sophtron-Integration/blob/main/js/lib/serviceClient.js), [Sophtron OAuth sample](https://github.com/sophtron/Sophtron-Integration/blob/main/js/lib/oauth.js), [Yodlee US open-banking guide](https://developer.yodlee.com/products/yodlee/us-open-banking/docs/product-guide)

## Implementation decision and follow-through

The initial source review was Plaid-specific: `convex/lib/plaidApi.ts` uses deployment-wide environment credentials, `convex/plaid.ts` derives `client_user_id` from authentication, and `convex/schema.ts` stores owned `plaidItems`, `plaidAccountId`, and `plaidTransactionId`. It had no user-credential table or provider selector. Public status redacts access tokens; anonymous/sample workspaces cannot connect real banks. See [existing Plaid contract](plaid.md).

Personal deployments can use the existing Plaid path, with accurate connection-limit/setup errors. A shared deployment should not infer remaining Trial slots from its active Items: deleted or externally created Items are missing from that count.

The [implemented Sophtron pilot](sophtron.md) is a **single-owner, deployment-configured adapter importing existing Sophtron-linked accounts**, enabled after the owner creates/authorizes their own provider account. Bind one configured Sophtron customer to one verified Marten user on the server. Enumerate only that customer's members/accounts; do not assign the first returned customer to whichever user clicks Connect. Fetch cached balances and posted transactions using the documented V2 routes and server-side HMAC authentication. Investment balances are included; holdings are outside this first pilot. Keep bank login/MFA in Sophtron's own interface for this initial pilot. [V2 API](https://docs.sophtron.com/?urls.primaryName=Sophtron-Api+V2+endpoints), [official HMAC example](https://github.com/sophtron/Sophtron-Integration/blob/main/js/lib/directAuth.js)

The adapter uses provider-scoped external IDs and connection ownership, preserves annotations/rules, distinguishes current and available balances, and requires explicit currency/debt-sign review. Because V2 does not document pending-to-posted association or deletion/completeness signals, the pilot imports only supported posted records and retains an explicit incomplete-history notice. Require explicit account mapping when changing providers so the same checking account or IRA cannot be counted twice. Unknown statement fields stay unknown. Validate disconnection, retries, persistence, and refresh freshness with consented real data; fixture success alone is insufficient.

This pilot now has focused fixture validation and import/setup UI. Its remaining gates are owner-controlled provider enrollment/consent and representative data validation. A shared Sophtron service needs its permitted usage/credentials arrangement settled; Yodlee additionally needs a concrete live-access offer. Yodlee remains research-only.
